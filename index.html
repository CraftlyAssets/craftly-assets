Licensing

Every asset ships with a commercial-use license. You can use it to make and sell finished products, physical or digital, without paying us anything extra.

You may not resell, share or redistribute the original files themselves — whether or not they have been modified.

About

Craftly Assets is a small independent design studio. Every asset is drawn and exported in-house, then checked for clean geometry, correct scale and sensible file sizes before it goes out. We build for people who actually use the files: cutters, printers, sellers and teachers.
